Our Story

Cooked with Heart

Miraz Kitchen was born from a simple belief: that the best meals come from home. Tucked away in Awada Layout, Onitsha, we've been feeding families, celebrating milestones, and making people feel welcome one plate at a time.

Every dish is made fresh daily using locally sourced produce. From our smoky Jollof Rice to our richly spiced Egusi Soup, every bite carries the warmth of Nigerian home cooking done right.
